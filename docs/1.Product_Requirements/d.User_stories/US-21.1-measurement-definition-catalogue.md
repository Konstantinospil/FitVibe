## User Story

**As a** system administrator  
**I want** to manage a canonical catalogue of biometric and performance measurement definitions  
**So that** users can record comparable measurements with consistent metadata

## Acceptance Criteria

- [US-21.1-AC01](../e.Acceptance_Criteria/US-21.1-AC01.md): Canonical catalogue identity and administration
- [US-21.1-AC02](../e.Acceptance_Criteria/US-21.1-AC02.md): Unit metadata and validation ranges
- [US-21.1-AC03](../e.Acceptance_Criteria/US-21.1-AC03.md): Historical reference stability

## Ownership Decision

Measurement definitions are global, system-owned catalogue records. Users may discover and select them, but creation of global definitions is an administrative operation. Administrative provenance is recorded through the audit log; catalogue rows intentionally do not carry user ownership or a creator foreign key.
