## Criterion

A newer authoritative publication can mark prior acceptance outdated and trigger re-acceptance; a minor publication remains auditable but does not by itself invalidate an existing acceptance.

## Evidence Required

- relevant API/database integration evidence;
- E2E evidence for user/admin workflow where applicable;
- audit/publication/acceptance record evidence;
- proof that historical published content cannot be mutated in place.
