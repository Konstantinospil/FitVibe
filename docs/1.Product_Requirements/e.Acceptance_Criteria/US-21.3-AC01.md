## Criterion

Authenticated users can search the complete canonical biometric or performance measurement catalogue, including definitions not currently shown on their profile. Search results expose enough metadata to identify each definition.

## Evidence Required

- frontend requests the measurement catalogue with `includeHidden=true`;
- optional text search is passed through the canonical `q` query;
- results expose label, description or granularity, category and visibility state;
- empty/loading/error states are explicit.
