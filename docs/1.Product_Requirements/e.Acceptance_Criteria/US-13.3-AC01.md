## Criterion

Every product interaction that can be completed by dragging also provides a non-dragging keyboard-operable method with equivalent result.

## Evidence Required

- criterion-specific automated test output where automation is meaningful;
- manual verification for behavior that automated tools cannot establish;
- no contradictory accessibility requirement in canonical documentation.

## Verification

- [ ] Criterion satisfied
- [ ] Required evidence linked
- [ ] No regression introduced in related accessibility behavior
