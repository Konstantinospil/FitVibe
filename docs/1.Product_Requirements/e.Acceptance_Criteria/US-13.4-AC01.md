## Criterion

Pointer targets satisfy WCAG 2.2 target-size requirements; any permitted exception is intentional, documented, and does not reduce keyboard accessibility.

## Evidence Required

- criterion-specific automated test output where automation is meaningful;
- manual verification for behavior that automated tools cannot establish;
