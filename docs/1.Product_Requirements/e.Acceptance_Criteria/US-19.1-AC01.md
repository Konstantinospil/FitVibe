## Criterion

Publishing Terms creates an immutable snapshot containing complete published content, publication metadata, actor, effective/version metadata, integrity metadata, and change classification (`editorial` or `material`) and explicit user effect (`none`, `acknowledge`, `accept`, or `renew_consent`).

## Evidence Required

- relevant API/database integration evidence;
- E2E evidence for user/admin workflow where applicable;
- audit/publication/acceptance record evidence;
- proof that historical published content cannot be mutated in place.
