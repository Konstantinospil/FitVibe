## Criterion

Authentication does not require CAPTCHA/puzzle-style cognitive tests; abuse resistance is implemented through non-cognitive controls consistent with NFR-001 and ADR-030.

## Evidence Required

- criterion-specific automated test output where automation is meaningful;
- manual verification for behavior that automated tools cannot establish;
- no contradictory accessibility requirement in canonical documentation.
