## Criterion

Registration cannot complete without explicit acceptance of the current authoritative Terms publication, and the acceptance record stores timestamp plus the accepted authoritative publication/version.

## Evidence Required

- relevant API/database integration evidence;
- E2E evidence for user/admin workflow where applicable;
- audit/publication/acceptance record evidence;
- proof that historical published content cannot be mutated in place.
